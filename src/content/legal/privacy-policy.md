This policy explains how Global Alpha Tech Security Pvt. Ltd. ("Global Alpha Tech", "we", "us") handles personal information collected through globalalphatech.com. We have written it to be read, not skimmed past.

## What we collect

**Information you give us.** When you send an enquiry or a job application, we receive what you type into the form: usually your name, email address, phone number, business name, the service you are interested in, your budget range and your message. Job applicants also share their city, experience and links to a CV or portfolio.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to keep the site running. If we add analytics or advertising tools, they may set cookies; see "Cookies" below.

We do not ask for, and do not want, sensitive information such as financial details, health information or government identity numbers through this website.

## How we use it

- To reply to your enquiry and discuss the work you are interested in
- To assess job applications and contact candidates
- To keep the website secure and working properly
- To understand, in aggregate, how the website is used so we can improve it

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not add enquiry details to marketing lists without your agreement.

## Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run the website and our business, such as our web hosting and email providers, and only as far as they need it to provide that service. We may also disclose information if the law requires us to.

## How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to respond and, if we work together, for the duration of that relationship and any period required for legal or accounting reasons. Job applications are kept for up to 12 months so we can contact you if a suitable role opens, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

## Cookies

The website itself works without cookies. Embedded content, such as the Google Map on our contact page, may set cookies from the provider that serves it. If we later add analytics or advertising tools that use cookies, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

## Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other applicable laws, you can ask us to access, correct or erase the personal information we hold about you, and you can withdraw consent you have given. To make a request, email [info@globalalphatech.com](mailto:info@globalalphatech.com). We will respond within a reasonable time.

## Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information, including encrypted connections to this website. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
