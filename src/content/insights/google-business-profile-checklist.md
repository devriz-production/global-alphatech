When someone nearby searches for "dentist near me" or "coaching centre in Laxmi Nagar", the businesses that appear on the map are chosen largely from their Google Business Profiles. For a local business it is often the single most valuable free listing you can have, and many profiles are left half finished.

Work through this checklist once, then come back to the "every month" section regularly.

## 1. The basics, done properly

- **Claim and verify the profile.** Search for your business on Google. If a profile exists, claim it; if not, create one at business.google.com and complete verification.
- **Use your real business name.** Do not add keywords or locations to the name. It breaks Google's guidelines and can get the profile suspended.
- **Choose the right primary category.** This affects which searches you appear in more than almost anything else. Pick the most specific category that describes your main service, then add a few relevant secondary categories.
- **Match your name, address and phone number everywhere.** The details on your website, social profiles and directories should be identical to your profile, down to the spelling.
- **Set accurate opening hours,** including special hours for festivals and holidays.

## 2. Make the profile persuasive

- **Write a clear description.** Say what you do, who you serve and what makes you different, in plain language.
- **Add your services or products,** with short descriptions and prices where it makes sense.
- **Upload real photos.** Your shopfront or building entrance (so people recognise it when they arrive), your interior, your team and your work. Add new photos regularly.
- **Link to the right page.** Point the website link to your homepage or a relevant location page, and add tracking parameters so you can see these visits in Google Analytics.
- **Add booking or enquiry links** if you take appointments online.

## 3. Reviews: the part most businesses neglect

- **Ask every happy customer.** Create a short review link from your profile and share it on WhatsApp or by email after a visit or purchase.
- **Reply to every review,** good and bad, within a few days. Thank people by name. For complaints, apologise, keep it short and offer to sort it out offline.
- **Never buy or swap reviews.** Google removes them, and they can get your profile suspended.

## 4. Every month

- Post an update, offer or event to keep the profile active.
- Add a few new photos.
- Check that your hours, phone number and services are still correct.
- Look at the performance report: how many people called, asked for directions or visited your website, and which searches found you.

## 5. Beyond the profile

Your profile works best with a website that supports it. A fast site with a clear page for each service, and a page for each area you serve, helps Google trust that you are the right answer for local searches. Mentions of your business on reputable local websites and directories help too.

## A quick self check

- [ ] Verified profile with the correct primary category
- [ ] Name, address and phone identical everywhere
- [ ] Hours, services and description complete
- [ ] At least 10 real, recent photos
- [ ] A simple way to ask for reviews, and replies to every review
- [ ] A monthly reminder to post and update
