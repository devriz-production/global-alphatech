When people search for the top digital marketing agency in Delhi, they are not looking for promises. They want proof: a clear strategy, real expertise and results they can measure. Delhi is one of the most crowded markets in India, and a business without a sharp digital partner gets buried quickly.

This guide covers what the best agencies have in common, the questions to ask before you sign anything, and how we work at Global Alpha Tech.

## What the best agencies in Delhi have in common

- **Return on investment over activity.** Posting every day and running ads is activity. A good agency starts from your numbers: what a customer is worth to you and what you can afford to pay to win one.
- **Everything under one roof.** Your website, search, ads, social media and email should work as one system. Five vendors usually means five plans pulling in different directions.
- **Plain, regular reporting.** Every month you should know what was spent, what it produced and what changes next.
- **Strong creative.** Delhi audiences scroll past ordinary ads. Good creative is not decoration; it is what makes the targeting pay off.
- **Fast websites built to convert.** A slow or confusing website wastes every rupee spent sending people to it.
- **Local understanding.** Customers in Delhi NCR are competitive, price aware and quick to compare. Campaigns should reflect that.

## What a full service agency should be able to cover

- **Google Ads:** Search, Performance Max, Shopping and YouTube campaigns
- **Meta Ads:** Facebook and Instagram, including reels and instant lead forms
- **SEO and content:** technical SEO, local SEO, articles and landing pages
- **Social media:** content calendars, reels, community management, influencer and UGC campaigns
- **Websites:** WordPress, Shopify and custom builds, landing pages and funnels
- **Branding:** logo, identity system and brand guidelines
- **Production:** product and model shoots, video editing and motion graphics
- **CRM and automation:** lead capture, WhatsApp follow up and reporting

You may not need all of these on day one. But an agency that can offer them can grow with you, without handing you over to someone new every time your needs change.

## Questions to ask before you sign

1. What results have you achieved for businesses like mine, and can I speak to one of those clients?
2. Who will actually work on my account, and who is my single point of contact?
3. What will you report on each month? Can I see a sample report?
4. Are the ad accounts, analytics and website set up in my name?
5. Is ad spend kept separate from your fee?
6. What exactly happens in the first 90 days?
7. How much notice do I need to give if I want to stop?

A good agency will answer every one of these clearly, and in writing.

## Red flags to watch for

- Guaranteed first page rankings, or a fixed number of leads, before they understand your business
- Reports full of impressions and likes, with nothing about enquiries or sales
- Ad accounts or your domain registered in the agency's name instead of yours
- Long lock in contracts before any results have been shown

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a digital marketing agency in Delhi cost?

It depends on the services, the competition in your market and how much is done for you. Ask every agency for a written quote that separates their fee from ad spend. You can read [how we price our work](/pricing/).

### How soon will I see results?

Paid ads can bring enquiries within days of launch. SEO and social media build over months. Be wary of anyone who promises guaranteed rankings.

### Can one agency handle both my website and my marketing?

Yes, and it usually works better. The team running your ads can improve the landing pages directly instead of waiting on another vendor.

### Do I need a big budget to start?

No. Many businesses start with one channel and a modest budget, prove that it works, and then scale.
